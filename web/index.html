Open kit for the Snapmaker U1 toolchanger

Rigid + TPU in one print, measured.

Pick a part, set your sizes, and download a 3MF with every material already on its toolhead. It runs in your browser with the project's own OpenSCAD files; nothing is uploaded.

Toolheads

    Open it in Snapmaker Orca, put the filaments on the toolheads shown above, and slice. Every part is already assigned.

    Why "measured"

    Will TPU stay bonded to PLA, PETG or ASA?

    U1 owners keep asking, and nobody has answered with numbers yet. rigidflex is the test campaign that does, run on the U1 and published as open data. It builds on six published studies of rigid–TPU interfaces and adds what they did not test: a toolchanger, ASA, heat, and three-material joints.

    180bond-test coupons in 10 plates, plus 6 functional parts
    3 × 2rigid materials (PLA, PETG, ASA) × TPU 95A and 85A
    6series: joint shape, lap and print order, temperature, heat soak, graded three-material joints, real parts
    100 %of the raw force–extension curves published, under CC BY 4.0

    The pull rig

    A motorised tensile tester for about 75–100 €

    • 50 kg S-type load cell read 80 times a second
    • Constant 2 mm/s from a NEMA17, a 3:1 belt and a self-locking T8 lead screw
    • ESP32 firmware that stops on its own when the coupon breaks and streams the whole curve over USB
    • Six printed parts, sized for ≈ 360 N with a 2.5× torque margin
